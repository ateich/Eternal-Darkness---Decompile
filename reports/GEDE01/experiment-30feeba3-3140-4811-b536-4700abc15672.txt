Distinct codegen hypothesis tested
==================================

Starting from the accepted 452-byte reconstruction, a second `changed = 1;`
was inserted as the first statement of the `enabled != 1` block.  The retail
instruction at function offset 0x108 is `li r31, 1`, so this tested whether the
apparently redundant source assignment would also cause MWCC to reuse CR0 from
the comparison at 0x100 for the discriminator selection at 0x10c.

Command:
  .tools/bin/ninja build/GEDE01/src/game/game_fn_801A94E4.o
  python3 tools/fndiff.py game/game_fn_801A94E4.c fn_801A94E4

Raw result:
  target size: 452 bytes
  generated size: 456 bytes
  objdiff match: 99.11504%

The compiler emitted the desired `li r31, 1` and then retained a fresh
`cmpwi r28, 1`.  The generated stream was therefore four bytes too long and
all following branch destinations shifted.  This hypothesis was rejected and
the source was restored to the non-regressing 452-byte reconstruction.  The
raw experiment objdiff is preserved in
`experiment-redundant-assignment-30feeba3-3140-4811-b536-4700abc15672.json`.
