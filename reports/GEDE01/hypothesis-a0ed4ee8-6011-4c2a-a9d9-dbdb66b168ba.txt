Attempt 4 codegen hypothesis and result

The earlier attempts tested ordinary pointer aliases, field-address expressions,
and several initializer prototype shapes. This attempt tested a distinct
register-lifetime hypothesis: declaring the address of the local descriptor as
an explicit register-qualified pointer might make GC/1.3 materialize the address
before assigning it to argument register r3. It also tested a volatile-qualified
initializer parameter, to determine whether qualifier-aware argument conversion
would inhibit address/result coalescing.

Both probes were compiled with the canonical GC/1.3 flags. The register-pointer
probe is preserved in probe-disasm.s and the volatile-parameter probe in
probe-volatile-disasm.s. Both produced the same 276-byte text and prologue:

  stw r31,204(r1)
  mr r31,r3
  addi r3,r1,8

Neither produces retail's earlier address computation through r0:

  addi r0,r1,8
  stw r31,204(r1)
  mr r31,r3
  mr r3,r0

The final source therefore retains the semantically precise typed prototype and
the existing honest NonMatching reconstruction. Canonical and relocation-strict
objdiff both measure 95.57143%. All seven relocation types, symbols, addends, and
ordering agree; generated relocation offsets remain four bytes earlier after the
missing prologue instruction.
