Attempt 4 codegen hypothesis
============================

The preserved best source uses a ternary expression to select discriminator
10 or 29.  Earlier work tested a second standalone `changed = 1` immediately
before that ternary.  This attempt instead expressed the selection as an
explicit if/else after a second `changed = 1`, testing whether MWCC's branch
lowering would reuse CR0 from the enclosing `enabled != 1` test across the
non-CR-setting `li r31, 1`.

The explicit if/else emitted the desired `li r31, 1` at function offset 0x108,
but MWCC still emitted a fresh `cmpwi r28, 1` at 0x10C.  The result was one
instruction too long and shifted all later branch destinations.  The variant
was rejected and the size-exact 452-byte reconstruction was restored.
